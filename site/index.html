Card Dashboard

Card Dashboard is a personal tool that one person, its owner, built and runs for their own use. It keeps track of the owner's credit-card spending.

  • It reads the card-notification emails that the owner's banks send to the owner's Gmail account.
  • It records each transaction in a Google Sheet that the owner owns, and suggests a category for it.
  • It shows the records on a web dashboard and accepts entries that the owner sends through a private LINE bot.

It runs as Google Apps Script projects under the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else, and other people cannot sign up for it.
